About Wire Watch

What this is

A daily read of 121 Indian outlets — English, business, independent, environmental, legal, right-of-centre, television, fact-checkers and the language press in twelve languages — built from headlines and links only. No article text is copied or republished; every link goes to the publisher.

How it works

  • Each outlet is read through Google News for the last 24 hours.
  • Headlines are filed under desks, regions, parties and politicians by keyword (desks.yaml). A headline can sit on several desks.
  • Headlines about the same event are grouped into a story by shared words. Stories are ranked by how many outlets, outlet types and languages carried them.
  • Vernacular headlines are shown and linked exactly as published. They are machine-translated internally — never displayed — so they can be filed under desks and matched with the same story in other languages. 3767 of 3767 vernacular headlines translated by Google Translate.

Corrections policy and right of reply

Wire Watch reports what other outlets published and makes no claim of its own. If a headline is mis-filed, an ownership entry is out of date, or a profile is incomplete or unfair, write in with the correction and a source; corrections are made openly and noted. Anyone named on a profile may send a response, which will be linked from that profile.

Limits

  • Keyword filing cannot read tone or intent — a headline naming a politician is not a claim about them.
  • Story grouping works on wording; two very differently worded headlines on one event can stay apart.
  • Google News returns at most 100 items per outlet per day, so the busiest outlets are sampled.
  • TV coverage is read from channels' web desks, not from broadcasts.
